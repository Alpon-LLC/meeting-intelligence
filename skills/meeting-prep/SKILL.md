# Meeting preparation
Use only the selected calendar event and documents explicitly supplied for it.
Write a short local agenda, attendee questions, and known decisions to confirm. Distinguish supplied facts from suggestions.
Do not search unrelated mailboxes, private team rosters or CRM systems. Do not invent prior commitments.
The [record] event marker means scheduling is requested only after participant consent. It is not proof of consent by itself.
Never sign in on behalf of a human, request their password or bypass MFA. Bot browser login and Calendar OAuth are separate.
